## Notes for Sebastian and Landon

**What this document does not do.** It is not a substitute for a company. If
you are trading as yourselves, a claim comes at you personally regardless of
what these terms say. An LLC plus general liability insurance protects you more
than any clause here.

**The three things most likely to cause you an actual problem**, in order:

1. **Earnings claims.** Anything that implies a typical member makes money. This
   is regulator territory, not just contract territory, and terms buried in an
   app will not fix a promise made on a sales page.
2. **A member's outreach.** Someone will cold-call a number they should not
   have, or ignore a "stop calling me". Section 5 puts that on them, but you
   should also be teaching it, not only disclaiming it.
3. **A false Site Check finding repeated to a business.** Section 6 covers it,
   and the product itself should keep failing quiet rather than guessing.

**Where the terms should also appear.** In the app at first run, on the sales
page before payment, and in the purchase receipt. One place is not enough.
